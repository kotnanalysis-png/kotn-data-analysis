What the numbers mean

One-second rate is the share of consecutive bids by an account on different listings with timestamps no more than one second apart, calculated within each scheduled-closing round. The 60-second distinct-item maximum is a rolling activity measure; it does not prove software-assisted bids.

Selection bias and account counts

Rates use cross-listing consecutive transitions as denominator. Accounts with very few bids can have unstable rates. A fast group selected for speed cannot be used as an independent control.
