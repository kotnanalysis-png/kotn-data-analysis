What can and cannot be concluded

The same pseudonymous account exhibits rapid cross-listing gaps in both cohorts. This is a reproducible timing pattern, not proof of automation, identity, coordination, or wrongdoing. Multi-tab bidding, page design and one-second timestamp precision remain relevant alternatives.

Account-level interpretation

Repeated rapid cross-listing activity appears in both rounds. It may be consistent with efficient multi-tab navigation, very fast manual bidding, scripting, or platform-side timestamp/bid-processing effects. These alternatives cannot be separated using one-second timestamps alone. A rapid bid sequence also does not indicate that the bidder owned, sold, or won those items.
