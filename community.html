Research and tool remain independently accessible

Public-facing materials are maintained here rather than being dependent on a social-media post. The methodology, bidder analysis, data-quality limitations, and source repository are available for independent inspection.

BidWatch highlights participation by accounts meeting a predefined high-frequency threshold. A highlighted bid is an observation, not a finding of misconduct.
