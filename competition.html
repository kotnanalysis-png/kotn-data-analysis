Competitive counterbidding and interpretation

Near-instant replies by different accounts on the same listing are rare in this sample. This weakens a claim that the observed high-frequency group is systematically responding immediately to competing bids. Rapid cross-listing activity is a separate pattern.

To test price pressure after closure, capture final prices and verified winning bidders. Merely noting that an account is currently outbid does not prove it intentionally increased a price.
