Scope and attribution. This report measures bidding activity, not bot identities. Fast bids, shared listings, and recurring sessions alone cannot establish automated bidding, operator affiliation, collusion, or misconduct. The auction operator's detection and enforcement practices are not available in these records.

Executive findings

  • High-speed cross-item bidding occurs. The busiest observed account placed 21 bids across 21 distinct items within a rolling 60-second window.
  • Rapid activity recurs. One previously discussed username, chikit1616, made 409 observed bids across 211 distinct listings, with 200 consecutive cross-item transitions of five seconds or less.
  • Immediate reactive counterbids are rare in this sample. Among 11,501 transitions to a different bidder on the same item, only 5 occurred within five seconds and 55 within thirty seconds.
  • Winning outcomes cannot be established. All catalogue records were marked open at capture time; a leading bid is not a final purchase.

Methods and reproducibility

The downloadable CSVs are transaction-level records, not derived summaries. The raw bids file preserves bid_id, listing_id, bidder, amount, and created_at; the catalogue CSV contains titles, last observed bids, leading usernames, and scheduling fields.

import pandas as pd
b = pd.read_csv('data/kotn_raw_bids_with_usernames.csv')
b['dt'] = pd.to_datetime(b['created_at'])
b = b.sort_values(['bidder', 'dt', 'bid_id'])
b['gap_seconds'] = b.groupby('bidder')['dt'].diff().dt.total_seconds()
b['previous_listing'] = b.groupby('bidder')['listing_id'].shift()
rapid = (b['gap_seconds'] <= 5) & (b['listing_id'] != b['previous_listing'])
print(b.loc[rapid].groupby('bidder').size().sort_values(ascending=False).head(10))

For same-listing response times, instead sort by listing_id, dt, then bid_id. Compare each bid's username with the preceding bid on that listing. Use transaction IDs to deduplicate across additional snapshots.

Limitations and next evidence

  • One-second timestamps cannot resolve subsecond bidding or distinguish software from a fast user interface.
  • This is a catalogue snapshot, not necessarily the complete lifetime activity of any account.
  • Recorded current leaders are not final winners. Closed auction outcomes and relistings need longitudinal captures.
  • To establish whether automation violates site policy, the standard UI's achievable throughput and site enforcement definitions must be examined.
  • The data does not identify individuals behind usernames, their relationship to KOTN, or private moderation actions.

Recommended next capture: preserve subsequent bid histories and final outcomes after auctions close, keeping snapshot timestamps and an immutable copy of each daily export.
