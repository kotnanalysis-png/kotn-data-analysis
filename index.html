Current evidence summary

153,777 recorded bids from 7,473 accounts across 52,784 bid-bearing listings. There are 2,833 consecutive cross-listing transitions within one second among 137,214 eligible transitions (2.06%).

Bid histories and snapshots are observations, not proof of automation or purchases. The November 9 collection is excluded from completed-day metrics.
