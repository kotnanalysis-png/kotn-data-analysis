Longitudinal study / October 2026

Two independent listing cohorts, compared using within-account consecutive bids on different items. This is a temporal replication check, not a test for bot activity.

Round 1: scheduled end Oct 18–19Round 2: scheduled end Oct 25–26Data through Oct 21
Important: Round 2 is still underway. October 19 was a transition day and is included when attached to the new closing cohort. The cohorts are defined by each listing's exported scheduled ending date, not by an assumption that all bids after October 18 belong to the new round. Scheduled close is not proof of final closure. Time stamps have one-second resolution. Any incomplete histories reduce observed counts.

Direct cohort comparison

Denominator: all consecutive same-account transitions between different listings in the same round. Each transition is assigned to the second bid in the pair. This avoids comparing raw one-second counts between differently sized samples.

Do the same accounts repeat the behaviour?

Returning accounts appear in both closing-date cohorts. The table shows their activity in each, ordered by first-round one-second count. Low-sample accounts must not be interpreted as changed behaviour based on percentages alone.

Long uninterrupted one-second sequences

Each streak contains at least three bids with consecutive cross-listing gaps of one second or less. A pair alone does not qualify. The timestamp precision means an apparent one-second gap may span any interval from almost zero to almost two seconds.
