How streaks and gaps are defined

Consecutive bids are ordered by timestamp and bid ID within the same account and closing cohort. A streak continues only while adjacent bids move to a different listing within one recorded second. Longer gaps break the streak. These are observed recorded timestamps, not confirmed UI click intervals.

What is a sequence?

A sequence requires at least three successive bids by the same account, each on a different listing than the immediately prior bid, with consecutive recorded gaps ≤1 second. This is stronger than counting isolated pairs, but does not prove automation.

Distinct patterns to test

Some high-speed accounts appear to move through nearby listing identifiers; others jump larger numeric distances. Future analysis should reconstruct the actual catalogue ordering by auction and time, and compare with volume-matched accounts before interpreting this as manual navigation or automation.
